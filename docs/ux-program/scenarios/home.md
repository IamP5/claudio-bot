# Home scenarios

Generated by `.claude/skills/verify-claudio-bot/bin/scenario.ts doc home` from `bin/scenarios.ts`; edit the registry, then regenerate. Each scenario answers, for one normal flow, whether the user is told the **state** honestly, can read it **clearly**, gets a **responsive** answer, and knows how to **recover** when something goes wrong.

Lanes: a `protocol` item is judged mechanically from the frames the phone receives (and the run's transcripts, files and pushes as independent views); a `simulator` item is judged by a person or agent from the named screenshots, taken with `--sim`. Every session turn runs on haiku; each permission card the scenario raises is answered Allow once, as the user would, and the timeline records the tap. A scenario whose preconditions fail is reported failed and its rubric is not judged; it never falls back to an easier path.

## H1 Unread and preview on the bot list

Needs the simulator: no (simulator items run with `--sim`).

**User journey.**

1. A new bot greets the user while they are on the list.
2. They open it, ask something, and go back to the list.
3. The reply lands; later they mark the chat unread to come back to it.

**Setup.**

- The phone sends a frame the app can send, built from what it saw: create_bot with ref `h1`. Mark `h1-create`.
- Wait up to 15 s for the created reply for `h1`; mark `h1-created`.
- Wait up to 180 s for `h1`'s first turn to end; mark `h1-ready`.
- Wait 2 s.
- Screenshot `h1-greeting` (simulator lane).
- Open the chat with `h1`: in the app by claudiobot://bot/<id> (simulator lane), or send `open` as the app does (protocol lane). Mark `h1-open`.
- The phone sends a frame the app can send, built from what it saw: send in `h1`: "Reply with one short sentence about tea.". Mark `h1-send`.
- The app goes back to the Sessions list (simulator lane: relaunched on the list; protocol lane: the phone unwatches every thread it watches).
- Wait up to 150 s for `h1`'s reply and the bot at rest; mark `h1-done`.
- Wait 2 s.
- Screenshot `h1-reply` (simulator lane).
- The phone sends a frame the app can send, built from what it saw: mark_unread on `h1`. Mark `h1-mark-unread`.
- Wait 2 s.
- The app goes back to the Sessions list (simulator lane: relaunched on the list; protocol lane: the phone unwatches every thread it watches).
- Screenshot `h1-marked` (simulator lane).

**Preconditions** (any failure fails the scenario):

- the reply arrived

## H2 The list while a bot works and then waits on the user

Needs the simulator: no (simulator items run with `--sim`).

**User journey.**

1. The user sends a bot a task and stays on the list.
2. The row shows what it does.
3. The bot asks a question; the row says it waits on them.
4. They answer.

- The phone sends a frame the app can send, built from what it saw: create_bot with ref `h2`. Mark `h2-create`.
- Wait up to 15 s for the created reply for `h2`; mark `h2-created`.
- Wait up to 180 s for `h2`'s first turn to end; mark `h2-ready`.
- The phone sends a frame the app can send, built from what it saw: send in `h2`: "Run `ls -la` and then `perl -e 'sleep 8'` with the Bash tool, then use your ask tool to ask me: Ship it today? with the options Yes and No. Then end your turn.". Mark `h2-send`.
- Wait 4 s.
- Screenshot `h2-working` (simulator lane).
- Wait up to 150 s for a question card from `h2`; mark `h2-card`.
- Wait 3 s.
- Screenshot `h2-card` (simulator lane).
- The phone sends a frame the app can send, built from what it saw: answer `h2`'s open question with its first option. Mark `h2-answer`.
- Wait up to 150 s for `h2`'s reply and the bot at rest; mark `h2-done`.

**Preconditions** (any failure fails the scenario):

- bot h2 was created
- a question card opened

**Rubric.**

| id | tells | lane | the user can tell | pass | fail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H2.1 | state | protocol | While the bot works, its row says what it is doing. | at least one activity frame with text for the bot between the send and the card | no activity: the row only says Typing… |
| H2.2 | state | protocol | When the bot waits on a question, the row says so and shows the question. | just before the user answers (3 s after the card), the bot's question is the card's and it is not working | another question on the row, or it still says working |

| H2.5 | state | simulator (h2-working) | The row of a working bot says it works and what it does. | a working mark and an activity line on the row | nothing tells the bot is busy |
| H2.6 | state | simulator (h2-card) | The row of a bot that waits on the user stands out and shows the question. | an attention badge and the question as the preview | the row looks like any other |

## H3 The Mac goes away for half a minute

Needs the simulator: no (simulator items run with `--sim`).

**User journey.**

1. The user has the bot list open.
2. The Mac drops off the network for 30 s.
3. It comes back.

**Setup.**

- The phone sends a frame the app can send, built from what it saw: create_bot with ref `h3`. Mark `h3-create`.
- Wait up to 15 s for the created reply for `h3`; mark `h3-created`.
- Wait up to 180 s for `h3`'s first turn to end; mark `h3-ready`.
- Screenshot `h3-online` (simulator lane).
- The network drops the phone (the socket closes); screenshot `h3-offline` 20 s into the drop (simulator lane); wait 30 s, reconnect with the stream position (epoch, after) and resume each thread from its last position. Mark `h3-back`.
- Wait 4 s.
- Screenshot `h3-back` (simulator lane).

**Preconditions** (any failure fails the scenario):

- bot h3 was created

**Rubric.**

| id | tells | lane | the user can tell | pass | fail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H3.1 | recover | protocol | When the Mac is back, the list is as it was: nothing lost, nothing doubled. | the hello after the drop lists the same bots with the same unread counts | a bot missing, doubled, or with a changed count |
| H3.2 | recover | simulator (h3-offline) | While the Mac is away, the list says so and offers a way out, and keeps the chats on screen. | a banner that the Mac is not answering, with Retry, and the list still drawn | no banner, a blank list, or Connected |
| H3.3 | state | simulator (h3-back) | When the Mac is back, the banner goes. | no banner, the list as before | a banner left over |
