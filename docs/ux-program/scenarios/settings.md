# Settings scenarios

Generated by `.claude/skills/verify-claudio-bot/bin/scenario.ts doc settings` from `bin/scenarios.ts`; edit the registry, then regenerate. Each scenario answers, for one normal flow, whether the user is told the **state** honestly, can read it **clearly**, gets a **responsive** answer, and knows how to **recover** when something goes wrong.

Lanes: a `protocol` item is judged mechanically from the frames the phone receives (and the run's transcripts, files and pushes as independent views); a `simulator` item is judged by a person or agent from the named screenshots, taken with `--sim`. Every session turn runs on haiku; each permission card the scenario raises is answered Allow once, as the user would, and the timeline records the tap. A scenario whose preconditions fail is reported failed and its rubric is not judged; it never falls back to an easier path.

## T1 Pair another device from Settings

Needs the simulator: no (simulator items run with `--sim`).

**User journey.**

1. The user opens Settings and taps Pair another device.
2. They leave the code on screen for a minute while they find the other phone.
3. On the protocol lane, the phone asks for a second code and the first is tried.

**Setup.**

- Tap "Settings" in the app (simulator lane; an XCUITest finds the element by its label, waits up to 10 s for it to be enabled and taps it; a disabled one fails the precondition); mark `t1-settings`.
- Screenshot `t1-settings` (simulator lane).
- Tap "Pair another device" in the app (simulator lane; an XCUITest finds the element by its label, waits up to 10 s for it to be enabled and taps it; a disabled one fails the precondition); mark `t1-sheet`.
- The label probe (the same read-only XCUITest) records, about once a second until the setup ends, every element of the app whose label holds "Expires", "expires", "expired", "Pairing QR code", and whether each button among them is enabled (simulator lane).
- Wait up to 15 s for the sheet's QR code; mark `t1-open` (optional).
- Screenshot `t1-code` (simulator lane).
- Wait 70 s.
- Screenshot `t1-later` (simulator lane).
- The phone sends a frame the app can send, built from what it saw: pair_code (Settings, Pair another device). Mark `t1-first`.
- Wait up to 10 s for the pair_code reply; mark `t1-first-in`.
- The phone sends a frame the app can send, built from what it saw: pair_code (Settings, Pair another device). Mark `t1-second`.
- Wait up to 10 s for the pair_code reply; mark `t1-second-in`.
- Another phone, "UX old-code phone", sends POST /pair with the first code. Mark `t1-old`.

**Preconditions** (any failure fails the scenario):

- two codes were given
- with --sim, the Pair another device sheet opened and showed a code

## T2 Confirm a fact a bot remembered, in dark mode

Needs the simulator: no (simulator items run with `--sim`).

**User journey.**

1. The user tells a bot something to remember.
2. Settings, Memory shows it as a proposal from that bot.
3. They confirm it.

**Setup.**

- The phone sends a frame the app can send, built from what it saw: create_bot with ref `t2`. Mark `t2-create`.
- Wait up to 15 s for the created reply for `t2`; mark `t2-created`.
- Wait up to 180 s for `t2`'s first turn to end; mark `t2-ready`.
- The phone sends a frame the app can send, built from what it saw: send in `t2`: "Remember this about me: my favorite tea is genmaicha. Use your memory tool to save it, then reply in one short sentence.". Mark `t2-send`.
- Wait up to 150 s for `t2`'s reply and the bot at rest; mark `t2-done`.
- The phone sends a frame the app can send, built from what it saw: memory. Mark `t2-memory`.
- Wait up to 10 s for an unconfirmed fact; mark `t2-proposed`.
- Open the chat with `t2`: in the app by claudiobot://bot/<id> (simulator lane), or send `open` as the app does (protocol lane). Mark `t2-open`.
- Screenshot `t2-chat` (simulator lane).
- The app goes back to the Sessions list (simulator lane: relaunched on the list; protocol lane: the phone unwatches every thread it watches).
- Tap "Settings" in the app (simulator lane; an XCUITest finds the element by its label, waits up to 10 s for it to be enabled and taps it; a disabled one fails the precondition).
- Tap "Memory" in the app (simulator lane; an XCUITest finds the element by its label, waits up to 10 s for it to be enabled and taps it; a disabled one fails the precondition).
- Screenshot `t2-memory` (simulator lane).
- The phone sends a frame the app can send, built from what it saw: confirm_fact for the proposal. Mark `t2-confirm`.
- Wait 3 s.

**Preconditions** (any failure fails the scenario):

- bot t2 was created
- the bot proposed a fact

**Rubric.**

| id | tells | lane | the user can tell | pass | fail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T2.1 | clarity | protocol | Memory says the fact is a proposal and which bot made it. | unconfirmed, with the bot as proposer and its name in by | shown as confirmed, or no proposer |
| T2.2 | responsive | protocol | Confirm takes at once. | a memory frame with the fact confirmed within 2 s | no change, or an error |
| T2.3 | clarity | simulator (t2-memory) | In dark mode, the Memory proposal's Confirm button is readable (backlog: the pill is blank). | a Confirm label visible on its fill, next to Forget | a blank or unreadable pill |
| T2.4 | clarity | simulator (t2-chat) | In dark mode, the chat's memory notice and its Confirm are readable. | the notice and its buttons read clearly | blank or low-contrast buttons |

## T3 Settings while the Mac stays away for over a minute

Needs the simulator: no (simulator items run with `--sim`).

**User journey.**

1. The user has Settings open.
2. The Mac drops off the network for 80 s.
3. It comes back.

**Setup.**

- Tap "Settings" in the app (simulator lane; an XCUITest finds the element by its label, waits up to 10 s for it to be enabled and taps it; a disabled one fails the precondition); mark `t3-settings`.
- The label probe (the same read-only XCUITest) records, about once a second until the setup ends, every element of the app whose label holds "Reconnecting", "Connected", "Connecting", "answering", "reach", "Offline", "offline", and whether each button among them is enabled (simulator lane).
- The network drops the phone (the socket closes); screenshot `t3-away` 70 s into the drop (simulator lane); wait 80 s, reconnect with the stream position (epoch, after) and resume each thread from its last position. Mark `t3-back`.
- Wait 10 s.
- Screenshot `t3-back` (simulator lane).

**Preconditions** (any failure fails the scenario):


**Rubric.**

| id | tells | lane | the user can tell | pass | fail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T3.1 | recover | protocol | After 80 s away, the phone picks up where it left off. | the hello after the drop resumed the stream | a fresh start: whatever happened while away is replaced, not replayed |
| T3.2 | state | simulator (list probe) | After a minute away, Settings stops saying Reconnecting and says the Mac is not answering (backlog). | no label reads Reconnecting from 62 s into the drop until it ends | Reconnecting still shown after a minute |
| T3.3 | responsive | simulator (list probe) | When the Mac is back, Settings says Connected soon. | a label reads Connected within 15 s after the network returns | no Connected within 15 s |
| T3.4 | recover | simulator (t3-away) | Away for over a minute, Settings tells the truth and offers a way out. | a status that says the Mac is not answering, and Change address | Reconnecting with nothing else to do |
